# Niva Bupa Health Insurance Policy Rules & Rejection Gotchas

## 1. Plan Overview (ReAssure 2.0 / Health Companion)
- Standard Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 to ₹1,00,00,000
- Pre-Hospitalization: 60 days
- Post-Hospitalization: 180 days
- ReAssure Forever / Lock the Clock benefits applicable on select variants

## 2. Key Sub-limits & Mandatory Policy Conditions
- **Room Rent**: Single Private Room (or No Sub-limit on ReAssure 2.0). Older Health Companion plans cap at 1% of Sum Insured per day.
- **Zone-Based Co-payment**: Policies purchased under Zone 2 (non-metros) or Zone 3 incur a **20% co-payment** if hospitalization occurs in a Zone 1 (Metro / Tier 1) hospital, unless the Zone Upgrade rider is active.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period**: 36 months default (reducible to 24 or 12 months with PED Buy-down rider).
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period**: 24 months for stones, hernia, joint replacement, and cataracts.

## 3. Real-World Claim Repudiation Traps (Avoidance Guide)
1. **Mid-Policy Habit or Lifestyle Changes**: If an insured individual starts smoking, using tobacco products, or develops high BP / diabetes after the policy was issued and does not declare it upon renewal endorsement, claims for related ailments can be contested by underwriters for material non-disclosure.
2. **Zone Differential Co-pay Surprise**: Treatment taken in Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, or Bengaluru without zone upgrade add-on causes an unexpected 20% co-pay deduction on the gross bill amount.
3. **Delayed Claim Submission**: Post-discharge reimbursement claims must be submitted within **30 days of discharge**. Late submissions without valid written medical justification (e.g. ICU stay or bed-ridden certificate) risk automatic rejection.
4. **Indoor Case Papers (ICP) Requirement**: For hospitalizations exceeding ₹1,00,000, Niva Bupa TPAs strictly demand hospital Indoor Case Papers (ICP), daily doctor notes, and temperature charts. Discharge summary alone is insufficient.
5. **Non-Payable Consumables**: Syringes, PPE kits, gloves, nebulization kits, and registration charges are deducted unless the Safeguard+ rider is active.

## 4. Statutory IRDAI Grievance Escalation
- First Step: File grievance directly with Niva Bupa Grievance Redressal Officer (gro@nivabupa.com)
- Second Step: If unresolved within 15 days, lodge an official complaint on the IRDAI Bima Bharosa Portal (https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in)
- Third Step: Approach Insurance Ombudsman in your jurisdiction within 1 year of final insurer rejection.
