# ICICI Lombard Health Insurance Policy Rules & Rejection Gotchas

## 1. Plan Overview (Health AdvantEdge / Complete Health Insurance)
- Standard Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 to ₹3,00,00,000
- Pre-Hospitalization: 60 days
- Post-Hospitalization: 180 days
- Reset Benefit: 100% reload of Sum Insured for unrelated illnesses

## 2. Key Sub-limits & Mandatory Policy Conditions
- **Room Category**: Single private room on basic plans; no room rent capping on higher sum insured (> ₹10 Lakhs).
- **Sub-limits on Specific Surgeries**: Cataract capped at ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 per eye depending on Sum Insured slab.
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period**: 24 months for stones, fibroids, hernia, and orthopedic joint replacements.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period**: 24 to 36 months continuous coverage.

## 3. Real-World Claim Repudiation Traps (Avoidance Guide)
1. **Unregistered or Ghost Hospital Trap**: The treating hospital must hold a valid, non-expired Clinical Establishments Act registration. ICICI Lombard strictly repudiates claims from blacklisted or de-paneled facilities.
2. **Investigation-Only Hospitalization Clause**: Admissions solely for CT Scans, endoscopy, MRI, or blood workups without active pharmacological therapy or surgical intervention are explicitly excluded under General Exclusion 1.
3. **Mid-Policy Addiction or Smoking Concealment**: If medical case notes or doctor's admission summary state "Chronic smoker for 5 years" while the policy proposal form stated "Non-smoker" 2 years prior, the entire claim will be rejected for non-disclosure of material facts.
4. **Original Bills & Pharmacy Break-up**: Every pharmacy expense above ₹500 requires a doctor's corresponding prescription showing drug dosage and duration. Pharmacy cash memos without doctor endorsement are disallowed.
5. **Reimbursement Submission Window**: Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of discharge. Intimation to the insurer must occur within 24 hours of emergency admission.

## 4. Statutory IRDAI Grievance Escalation
- First Step: ICICI Lombard Grievance Manager (customersupport@icicilombard.com)
- Second Step: IRDAI Bima Bharosa Portal (https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in)
- Third Step: Insurance Ombudsman redressal mechanism.
