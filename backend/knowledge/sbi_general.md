# SBI General Health Insurance Policy Rules & Rejection Gotchas

## 1. Plan Overview (Arogya Supreme / Arogya Plus / Arogya Premier)
- Standard Sum Insured: ₹3,00,000 to ₹50,00,000
- Pre-Hospitalization: 60 days
- Post-Hospitalization: 90 days
- OPD Benefit: Covered on select policy variants up to sub-limit

## 2. Key Sub-limits & Mandatory Policy Conditions
- **Room Rent**: Standard variants cap room rent at 1% of Sum Insured per day. ICU capped at 2% of Sum Insured. Exceeding room rent limits causes proportionate deductions across all linked medical bills.
- **Co-Payment Clause**: 20% co-payment applies for insured members aged 55 years or older entering into basic health policies, unless co-pay waiver is selected.
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period**: 24 months for benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia, hydrocele, fistula in ano, piles, sinusitis, and joint replacement.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period**: 36 to 48 months depending on product terms.

## 3. Real-World Claim Repudiation Traps (Avoidance Guide)
1. **OPD Claim Prescription Linkage**: OPD consultations and dental/optical claims under Arogya Plus require original itemized tax invoices accompanied by the treating physician's written prescription and diagnostic requisition.
2. **Preventive Health Checkup Expiry**: Annual preventive checkup vouchers expire on policy anniversary date and cannot be claimed retroactively or rolled over to the next policy year.
3. **Hospital Criteria Clause**: Hospital must have at least 15 in-patient beds (10 in towns with population < 10,000) with qualified nursing staff around the clock. Claims from clinics are rejected.
4. **Failure to Report Pre-Existing Symptoms**: Inconsistencies between the emergency admission questionnaire and proposal disclosures lead to immediate underwriter queries.
5. **30-Day Filing Limit**: Formal submission of claim form Part A and Part B with all original payment receipts within **30 days** of discharge.

## 4. Statutory IRDAI Grievance Escalation
- First Step: SBI General Grievance Cell (gro@sbigeneral.in)
- Second Step: IRDAI Bima Bharosa Portal (https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in)
- Third Step: Insurance Ombudsman.
