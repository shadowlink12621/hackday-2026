# Star Health Insurance Policy Rules & Rejection Gotchas

## 1. Plan Overview (Family Health Optima / Star Comprehensive)
- Standard Sum Insured: ₹3,00,000 to ₹25,00,000
- Pre-Hospitalization: 60 days
- Post-Hospitalization: 90 days

## 2. Key Sub-limits & Mandatory Policy Conditions
- **Room Rent Cap**: Capped strictly at 1% of Sum Insured or ₹5,00,000/day (whichever is lower) for standard plans.
- **Proportional Deduction**: Selecting a Deluxe/Suite room triggers proportional deductions across the whole hospital bill, resulting in 30-50% out-of-pocket loss!
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period**: 24 months for cataracts, hernia, hydrocele, joint replacement, hysterectomy, and gall bladder surgery.
- **Cataract Limit**: Capped at ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 per eye depending on Sum Insured tier.

## 3. Real-World Claim Repudiation Traps (Avoidance Guide)
1. **Unregistered Day-Care Center Trap**: Chemotherapy or dialysis performed at small day-care centers without a 24/7 ICU or round-the-clock nursing staff often gets rejected under Clause 3.1.
2. **First Consultation Paper Requirement**: Star Health requires the very first doctor prescription/OPD slip where symptoms were recorded. Missing the first prescription often stalls the claim under "unexplained onset duration".
3. **Investigation Only Hospitalization**: Hospitalization primarily for diagnostic tests or MRI/CT scan without active medical management is explicitly excluded.
4. **Claim Submission Deadline**: Must notify TPA/Insurer within 24 hours of emergency admission, and submit reimbursement papers within **15 days of discharge**.

## 4. Statutory IRDAI Grievance Escalation
- First Step: Star Health Grievance Redressal Officer (gro@starhealth.in)
- Second Step: IRDAI Bima Bharosa Portal (https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in)
- Third Step: Insurance Ombudsman redressal mechanism.
