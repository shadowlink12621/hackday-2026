# HDFC ERGO Health Insurance Policy Rules & Rejection Gotchas

## 1. Plan Overview (Optima Secure / My:Health Medisure)
- Standard Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 to ₹1,00,00,000
- Pre-Hospitalization: 60 days
- Post-Hospitalization: 180 days

## 2. Key Sub-limits & Mandatory Policy Conditions
- **Hospital Bed Requirement**: The treating facility MUST be registered with local authorities and have at least 15 in-patient beds in towns with population > 100,000, or 10 beds elsewhere. Day-care clinics must have fully equipped OT.
- **Room Rent**: No room rent sub-limit on Optima Secure; however, basic plans cap at 1% of Sum Insured per day. If exceeded, **Proportional Deduction** applies to all doctor fees, surgery, and diagnostics.
- **Initial Waiting Period**: 30 days for any non-accidental illness.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period**: 36 months (3 years) of continuous coverage.

## 3. Real-World Claim Repudiation Traps (Avoidance Guide)
1. **Lifestyle Disclosure Trap**: If the insured took up smoking or tobacco use *after* policy inception and did not notify the insurer during renewal, claims relating to respiratory or cardiovascular conditions are frequently denied citing non-disclosure of material facts.
2. **Missing Hospital Registration Certificate**: For claims from smaller private nursing homes, HDFC ERGO mandates submitting a copy of the hospital's municipal registration certificate and doctor qualification degrees.
3. **Non-Medical Consumables**: Gloves, PPE kits, admission kit, thermometers, and sanitizers are non-payable under standard policies unless the 'Consumables Add-on' was purchased.
4. **Claim Submission Deadline**: Reimbursement claim documents must be submitted within **30 days from the date of discharge**.

## 4. Statutory IRDAI Grievance Escalation
- First Step: HDFC ERGO Grievance Redressal Officer (grievance@hdfcergo.com)
- Second Step: IRDAI Bima Bharosa Portal (https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in)
- Third Step: Insurance Ombudsman redressal mechanism.
