# Care Health Insurance Policy Rules & Rejection Gotchas

## 1. Plan Overview (Care Supreme / Care Classic / Care Advantage)
- Standard Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 to ₹1,00,00,000
- Pre-Hospitalization: 60 days
- Post-Hospitalization: 180 days
- Cumulative Bonus (No Claim Bonus): Up to 500% with Care Shield

## 2. Key Sub-limits & Mandatory Policy Conditions
- **Room Category Restriction**: Care Classic caps room rent at Single Private Room or 1% of Sum Insured. Upgrading to Deluxe or Suite triggers **proportional deduction** on doctor rounds, surgery charges, and nursing fees.
- **Specific Illness Waiting Period**: 24 months continuous coverage mandatory for arthritis, gout, calculus (stones), hernia, hydrocele, benign prostatic hypertrophy, and prolapsed intervertebral disc (PIVD).
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period**: 36 months (reducible to 12 or 24 months with Instant Cover rider).
- **Day Care Treatments**: Must strictly qualify as surgical procedures performed under general/local anesthesia requiring less than 24 hours hospitalization due to advanced medical technology. Purely diagnostic procedures are non-payable.

## 3. Real-World Claim Repudiation Traps (Avoidance Guide)
1. **Hospital Bed Count Minimum**: The nursing home must possess at least 15 in-patient beds (10 beds in non-metro towns) and a dedicated fully equipped operation theatre. Claims from clinics or doctor residences are repudiated immediately.
2. **Failure to Submit First Prescription**: Claims for acute infections or fever (dengue, typhoid) require the first consultation prescription showing the exact date of symptom onset. If doctor notes state "symptoms since 2 months" on a policy active for 1 month, claim is rejected for pre-existing non-disclosure.
3. **Consumables Deduction**: Without the "Care Shield" add-on, approximately 8% to 15% of the total hospital bill (gloves, cotton, aprons, pulse oximeter probes) will be deducted as non-payable expenses.
4. **Sub-limit on Modern Robotic Surgeries**: Robotic prostatectomy or cyberknife procedures have product sub-limits (typically capped at ₹1,50,000 to ₹2,50,000).
5. **30-Day Document Filing Deadline**: Reimbursement claim forms and original invoices must reach the insurer/TPA within **30 calendar days** from the patient's discharge date.

## 4. Statutory IRDAI Grievance Escalation
- First Step: Write to Care Health Customer Services & GRO (customerfirst@careinsurance.com)
- Second Step: Escalate to IRDAI Bima Bharosa Portal (https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in)
- Third Step: Insurance Ombudsman hearing (free, legally binding on insurer up to ₹50,00,000).
